# Chen Sheng Hao at TEATOPIA 2026

Animated HTML slide deck for the 11-minute Chen Sheng Hao brand talk at
TEATOPIA Global Dialogue, Westin Bayshore, Vancouver — October 31, 2026.

- `index.html` — self-contained animated deck (12 slides, press N for speaker notes, T for the 11:00 timer)
- Slides 2, 4 and 10 are click-through: slide 2 moves the caravan along the Ancient Tea Horse Road stop by stop; slide 4 tells the founder's Lao Ban Zhang promise step by step; slide 10 plays two short motion-graphics clips, cspuerh.com and the 2026 Northwest Tea Festival, one per click (← steps back). Clips are H.264, so present in Chrome, Safari or Edge
